About the registry

Café Variome V4 enables secure, federated discovery of genomic and health data. Each institution runs its own node and keeps custody of its data; queries travel out to participating sources and come back only at the level the data owner has approved.

Nodes join networks to form focused discovery networks. Those networks are local and not controlled by any central authority, so they are often hard to find: you need to know which node to ask and which network you want. This registry lets Café Variome nodes list their networks, so people outside a project can find and join them.

List your network

A Café Variome node registers a network through the API when its owner chooses to list it. A registration carries a proof-of-work solution, and later changes are signed with the network's management key. New registrations may wait for an administrator's approval before they appear in the list.
